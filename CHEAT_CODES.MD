# Cheat codes

The PC game's cheat codes, entered with 3DS buttons.

## How to enter a code

On the title screen ("Please press START to begin"), press the code's eight buttons in
order. The cheat sound plays when it works. Enter as many codes as you like, then press
START.

A wrong press doesn't need clearing: just start the code again. A code lasts until you
close the game and isn't saved, except the cash codes, whose money stays in the career it
starts.

## Where they apply

As on the PC:

- **Parts** are for Quick Race and Customize on the main menu, not the career shops.
- **Sponsor cars** appear in Quick Race's car select: press R until SPONSOR CARS.
- **Vinyls** appear in every vinyl list, the career shops' included.
- **The RX-8 and Skyline** no longer wait for their career reward race in Quick Race and
  Customize.
- **Cash** is added to every new career's starting money; both codes together give $1,200.
